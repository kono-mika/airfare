# Code of conduct

AirFare's issues, pull requests and discussions are for building a useful tool together. Everyone taking part
is expected to:

* be respectful and patient, also when you disagree;
* keep the discussion about the work, not the person;
* welcome questions and people who are new.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal attacks, sharing anyone's
private information, and spam or promotion unrelated to the project.

## Reporting

To report a comment to GitHub's moderators, use \*\*Report content\*\* in its \*\*⋯\*\* menu.  To reach the
maintainer about anything else, open an issue asking for a private without the details.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock or close threads, and block people who don't follow this code.
